ROI mechanics

How the daily ROI is calculated.

The plan uses the total approved deposits that have completed the 24-hour wait. It is not based on an unrelated contract balance.

Formula
Eligible deposits × 2% = daily profit
Only approved deposits older than 24 hours are included. Profit is credited at most once per 24-hour cycle and recorded as a ledger entry.
$1,000 × 2% = $20

Compounding note: if profit is withdrawn, the eligible deposit base does not grow from that profit. Adding a new approved deposit can increase the base.

Security & controls

Protection around the account and the payout.

The platform combines access controls, administrator review and server-side key isolation.

Controls
01

KYC before withdrawal

Identity verification must be approved before withdrawal access is enabled.

02

2FA & session security

Optional TOTP two-factor authentication, hashed passwords and HTTP-only signed sessions.

03

Admin approval lock

A withdrawal moves from pending to processing under an atomic lock, preventing double approval.

04

Server-side key isolation

The BSC signing key is read only by the server-side withdrawal service and never shown in the browser.

On-chain verification: a broadcast transfer is only marked completed after the configured confirmation. A reverted or unverified transfer is not treated as paid.
